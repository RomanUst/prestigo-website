**Prague in December** is cold, dark by four o'clock and at its most beautiful: the Christmas markets are lit, the churches fill with Advent concerts and the Old Town smells of smoked ham and mulled wine. Expect daytime temperatures just above freezing, about eight hours of daylight and three public holidays in a row from 24 December. This guide covers what visitors from overseas need to plan around: the weather, the market calendar, what actually closes over Christmas, which day trips still work in winter and how to get around comfortably once the sun has set.

## Prague in December at a glance

- **Weather:** highs around 2–4 °C, lows around −2–0 °C. Damp more often than snowy; icy cobbles are common.
- **Daylight:** sunrise around 07:45–08:00, sunset around 16:00. The shortest day is 21 December.
- **Christmas markets:** Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square from 28 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, usually 10:00–22:00.
- **Advent Sundays:** 29 November, 6, 13 and 20 December. Weekends are the busiest days in the centre.
- **St Nicholas Eve:** the evening of 5 December, with costumed Nicholas, angel and devil groups on the streets.
- **Public holidays:** 24, 25 and 26 December, then 1 January. Large shops close at noon on the 24th and stay shut on the 25th and 26th.
- **Busiest travel days:** roughly 18–23 December and 27 December–2 January.

## What December weather in Prague is really like

December in Prague is grey rather than picture-postcard white. Most days sit between just below freezing and a few degrees above, with light rain, sleet or drizzle more likely than deep snow. Real snowfall happens in some years but rarely lasts in the city.

The damp cold of the river valley and the 16:00 sunset shape the day more than the temperature itself:

- **Sightsee in daylight.** Prague Castle, Charles Bridge and the Old Town lanes are best between 10:00 and 15:30. Save the markets, concerts and dinner for after dark, when the lights are on.
- **Dress for standing still.** A market evening means an hour or two outdoors without moving much. Thermal layers, a warm hat and gloves matter more than a heavy coat.
- **Wear shoes with grip.** The cobbles of the Old Town and Malá Strana turn slick in drizzle and frost, and the hill up to the castle is steep.

## Christmas markets in Prague: where and when

The two main markets are on **Old Town Square**, with the big tree in front of the Týn Church, and on **Wenceslas Square**. For 2026 they are listed from **28 November 2026 to 6 January 2027**, generally open from 10:00 to 22:00, with shorter hours expected on 24 and 31 December. The tree is usually lit for the first time at dusk on opening day.

Smaller neighbourhood markets are quieter and often better for food:

- **Náměstí Míru** in Vinohrady, in front of the Church of St Ludmila, popular with residents.
- **Náměstí Republiky**, next to the Municipal House and the Palladium shopping centre.
- **Tylovo náměstí**, a short walk from Náměstí Míru.

These open in late November and usually close around Christmas Eve, so plan them for the first three weeks of December. Organisers confirm exact dates each autumn.

## Christmas Eve, holidays and what closes

This is the part most December guides leave out. In the Czech Republic, **Christmas Eve (24 December)** is the main family celebration, not Christmas Day. Dinner of fried carp and potato salad is eaten at home, and the city goes quiet from mid-afternoon.

- **24 December:** shops with a sales area over 200 m² must close at **12:00** by law. Many restaurants close for the evening; hotel restaurants and some places in the centre stay open, usually with a set menu and advance booking.
- **25 and 26 December:** both are public holidays. Large shops are closed all day. The main markets stay open; many museums close on the 24th and some also on the 25th, so check the day before.
- **1 January:** a public holiday. Large shops are closed again and the city wakes up slowly. Our [New Year's Eve in Prague guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/new-years-eve-prague-2026) covers the night itself: fireworks rules, road closures and getting home after midnight.

Small shops, pharmacies and shops at the airport and the main railway station are exempt from the closures. Public transport runs throughout, on a holiday timetable. In practice: buy gifts before noon on the 24th, book Christmas Eve dinner well in advance, and if you have a flight on 24 or 25 December, confirm your airport transfer early rather than counting on finding a car on the day.

## Things to do in Prague in December beyond the markets

- **St Nicholas Eve, 5 December.** In the evening, groups dressed as St Nicholas, an angel and a devil walk the Old Town and give sweets to children who recite a poem. Lovely for families; the devils are theatrical rather than frightening.
- **Advent and Christmas concerts.** Churches across the centre host concerts of Baroque and Czech Christmas music through December, including Jakub Jan Ryba's *Czech Christmas Mass*, which is performed widely in Prague every year. Book seats for weekend dates ahead.
- **Quieter sights.** Early December is one of the calmest times to see Prague Castle, the Strahov Library and the Jewish Quarter without summer queues. Things get busier from mid-month.
- **Winter food.** *Svařák* (mulled wine), roasted chestnuts, *klobása* sausages and the Christmas biscuits called *cukroví*.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is December a good time to visit Prague?**
Yes, for atmosphere. The markets, lights and concerts are at their best, and early December is quieter than the last two weeks. Expect cold, short days and plan sightseeing before 16:00.

**Does it snow in Prague in December?**
Sometimes, but not reliably. Damp, grey days around freezing are more typical than deep snow, and snow in the city rarely lasts long.

**When do the Prague Christmas markets open in 2026?**
The main markets on Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square are listed from 28 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, usually 10:00–22:00, with shorter hours on 24 and 31 December.

**Are shops open on Christmas Eve in Prague?**
Only in the morning. By law, shops over 200 m² close at 12:00 on 24 December and stay closed on 25 and 26 December. Small shops, pharmacies and shops at the airport and main station can open.

**Are restaurants open on Christmas Eve?**
Many close in the evening because Czechs celebrate at home. Hotel restaurants and some places in the centre open with a set menu, so book ahead.

**What should I wear in Prague in December?**
Warm layers, a hat, gloves and waterproof shoes with good grip. Evenings at the markets mean standing outdoors for an hour or two.

**Can a car drive to Old Town Square?**
No. The square and most surrounding lanes are pedestrian. A chauffeur drops you and collects you at an agreed point at the edge of the zone.

**How early should I book an airport transfer for Christmas?**
As soon as your flights are confirmed, ideally by November. The days just before Christmas and around New Year are the busiest of the winter.
