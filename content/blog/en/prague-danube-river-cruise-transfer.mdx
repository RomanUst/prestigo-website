A **Prague to Danube river cruise transfer** by private car takes two and a half to three hours: about 220 km to Passau, a little further to Vilshofen, 285 km to Regensburg and 300 km to Nuremberg. There is no direct train from Prague to Passau or Vilshofen, so the car is the one option that takes you and a week's luggage from the hotel door to the gangway without a change. The detail most guides skip is the last kilometre: "Passau" on a cruise ticket can mean three different landing areas, and the river sometimes moves the embarkation port a few days before sailing. ## Which port does your ship really leave from?

Start with the embarkation line in your cruise documents, not the name of the itinerary. For 2026 the pattern on the upper Danube is this:

- **Passau** is the most common start and end point for one-week sailings to Vienna and Budapest.
- **Vilshofen** is the long-standing embarkation port of AmaWaterways on this stretch; the line has used the town for more than a decade, and a new dock was opened there for river cruise ships.
- **Regensburg** is listed as the starting port for Viking's Romantic Danube and for Uniworld's S.S. Beatrice sailings.
- **Nuremberg** is where several Christmas-market itineraries begin, including Advent sailings listed by AmaWaterways and Avalon for late November and December 2026.

If you bought the cruise line's Prague extension, its coach takes you to the ship. If you booked Prague yourself, added nights, or want to stop on the way, the transfer is yours to arrange.

## Passau: three landing areas, and one is not in the city

Passau's port is run by the municipal utility, Stadtwerke Passau, and it is large: 17 landing stages with 29 berths for passenger ships. They fall into three areas.

- **Altstadt (Old Town quay).** Berths A1 to A14 line the Danube bank of the old town, a few minutes on foot from the cathedral. A car can stop on the riverside road beside the ship.
- **Lindau.** The Lände Lindau is 3 km downstream, outside the city. Cruise lines usually run a shuttle into town from here, but your luggage still has to reach the quay.
- **Racklau.** The winter harbour at Racklau is about 2 km upstream, also beyond walking distance.

Stadtwerke Passau publishes a berth plan online that shows each ship's berth with its arrival and departure time. The code is simple: **A** is the Old Town, **L** is Lindau, and the number is the berth. Send us the ship's name when you book. Your chauffeur checks the plan on the morning of the transfer and drives to that berth.

## Vilshofen, Regensburg and Nuremberg: where the car stops

**Vilshofen an der Donau** is a small town a little over 20 km upstream of Passau. Ships tie up on the Danube promenade, close to the town square, and the car stops at the quay.

**Regensburg** berths its ships along the old-town bank of the Danube, around the 12th-century Stone Bridge. The medieval centre behind the quay is largely closed to traffic, so your chauffeur uses the riverside road and carries the cases to the gangway. The drive follows the D5 through Plzeň and the A93 south; details are on our [Prague to Regensburg route](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/prague-regensburg).

**Nuremberg** is different: river ships do not come into the old town. They use the city's passenger port at **Europakai 10**, on the Main–Danube Canal several kilometres to the south. Ask for the port, not the centre, when you book the [Prague to Nuremberg transfer](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/prague-nuremberg).

## When to leave Prague on embarkation day

Work backwards from the boarding window in your documents. Most lines open boarding in the afternoon; many will take your luggage earlier.

- **From a Prague hotel.** Leave at 09:30 for Passau and you are at the ship around 12:30, with time for lunch in the old town before boarding. For Regensburg or Nuremberg, 09:00 gives the same margin.
- **Straight from Prague Airport.** If your flight lands by late morning, the car can take you directly to the ship. Your chauffeur tracks the flight and waits in the arrivals hall for up to 60 minutes after landing. After an overnight long-haul flight, a night in Prague first is the calmer plan; our [airport transfer service](https://rideprestigo.com/services/airport-transfer) covers that leg.
- **The border.** The checks are made on entry to Germany and are usually brief, but they can add 10 to 20 minutes on a busy day.
- **Advent sailings.** From late November the road to Passau crosses the Šumava at Strážný/Philippsreut, where snow can lie while Prague is dry, and it is dark by half past four. Your chauffeur checks the pass before departure and takes the longer route through Austria if it is difficult. ## If the river moves your port: low and high water

The Danube between Straubing and Vilshofen is free-flowing and shallow, and it is the stretch that causes most changes. When the water is too low or too high for a ship to pass, lines move embarkation from Regensburg to Vilshofen or Passau, swap passengers between two ships by coach, or berth at Lindau or across the Austrian border at Engelhartszell in place of the Passau Old Town. Notice is often only a few days.

With a private transfer the fix is one message: tell us the new port as soon as the cruise line tells you. We change the destination and the pickup time, and confirm the quote again if the distance is different.

## A stop on the way: Český Krumlov or Plzeň

With a late-afternoon boarding there is room for one stop.

- **Towards Passau or Vilshofen: Český Krumlov.** The UNESCO town lies east of the direct road, about two and a half hours from Prague, with roughly two more hours on to Passau. With two to three hours in the town it is a full day, so leave by 08:00; see our [Český Krumlov guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-to-cesky-krumlov-private-day-trip).
- **Towards Regensburg or Nuremberg: Plzeň.** The city is on the D5, about an hour from Prague, and the Pilsner Urquell brewery tour fits a stop of two to three hours.

Mention the stop when you book, so that it is part of the fixed quote.

## After the cruise: from the ship to Prague or to your flight

Ships that end in Passau, Vilshofen or Regensburg ask guests to leave after breakfast, usually by 09:00. Your chauffeur meets you at the gangway, loads the luggage and has you at a Prague hotel around midday; see [Passau to Prague](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/passau-prague).

If you fly home the same day, count carefully. An 08:30 pickup in Passau reaches Prague Airport between 11:15 and 11:30. The airport asks for two hours before a Schengen flight and two and a half to three before a long-haul one, so book a departure after 14:00. Our [guide to departures from Prague Airport](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-airport-departure-transfer) explains terminals and drop-off.

## Which car for cruise luggage

Cruise passengers usually carry one large case and one cabin bag each. An **E-Class** suits a couple; an **S-Class** has the same capacity with an executive rear cabin. Two couples travelling together, or a family, need the **V-Class**, which takes six passengers and six large bags.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is the transfer from Prague to a Danube river cruise?**
About 2.5 to 3 hours to Passau (220 km), about 3 hours to Vilshofen, Regensburg (285 km) or Nuremberg (300 km), and about 2.5 hours to Linz (235 km).

**Where do river cruise ships dock in Passau?**
In one of three areas: the Old Town quay on the Danube (berths A1–A14), Lindau 3 km downstream, or Racklau about 2 km upstream. Stadtwerke Passau publishes the berth plan online, and your chauffeur checks it on the day.

**Is Vilshofen the same as Passau?**
No. Vilshofen an der Donau is a separate town a little over 20 km upstream of Passau. Some lines, AmaWaterways among them, embark there. Check which of the two your documents name.

**Is there a train from Prague to Passau or Vilshofen?**
There is no direct train. Rail connections need at least one change and take considerably longer than the drive.

**What if the cruise line changes the embarkation port?**
Tell us the new port and time. Low or high water sometimes moves embarkation between Regensburg, Vilshofen and Passau at short notice. We change the destination and confirm the quote again if the distance changes.

**Do we need passports for the drive from Prague to Germany?**
Yes. Both countries are in Schengen, but Germany is running temporary checks at its land borders until at least 15 March 2027. Carry passports, and visas where they apply, in the cabin of the car.

**How early should we leave Prague to board a ship in Passau?**
For an afternoon boarding, leave your hotel by 09:30. You reach the ship around 12:30, with a margin for the border and time for lunch before boarding opens. For Regensburg or Nuremberg, leave by 09:00.
