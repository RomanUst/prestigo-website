**A note on tickets.** Opera Ball tickets are sold only through the Opera Ball Office of the Vienna State Opera, and entrance tickets for 2027 were allocated by a draw that closed in June 2026. If you do not hold one, look at the Hofburg and Musikverein balls instead: they are just as formal, easier to enter, and each organiser sells through its own website. Some categories are already sold out, so check before you plan the journey. We drive you to the ball; we do not sell tickets.

**Dress code.** The Opera Ball is the strictest: a floor-length ball gown for women, a black tailcoat with white bow tie for men, or a gala uniform. At the Kaffeesiederball a tuxedo is accepted alongside tails. No ball has a changing room worth the name, so you arrive dressed.

## From Prague to a Vienna ball: timing the day

The drive is simple: the D1 from Prague to Brno, the D52 south through the Moravian wine country to the border at Mikulov, then the Austrian A5 into the city. In normal conditions it takes three and a half hours.

Work backwards from the doors. The Opera Ball admits guests from 20:15 and the opening ceremony begins at 22:00. The Kaffeesiederball opens its doors at 19:30 at the Heldenplatz entrance of the Hofburg, with the opening at 21:00. Hair, make-up and dressing take most people two to three hours, and a light early dinner is wise before a night that ends near dawn. That puts your arrival at the Vienna hotel at 15:00 at the latest.

- **Depart Prague at 10:30–11:00.** You reach Vienna around 14:30, with a margin for January weather on the D1.
- **Travelling the day before is calmer.** You wake up in Vienna, the dress hangs overnight, and a delay on the road costs nothing.
- **Friday departures need extra time.** Friday afternoon traffic out of Prague can add 20 to 30 minutes; for the Friday balls, leave in the morning.

Your gown and tailcoat travel flat or on a hanger, not folded in a suitcase. Tell us what you are carrying when you book and we plan the boot around it.

**On the night itself**, the car takes you from the hotel to the entrance of the State Opera, the Hofburg or the Musikverein, and collects you at the hour you choose. This matters more than it sounds. Traffic around the venues is heavy on ball nights, taxis are in short supply when a ball ends, and the U-Bahn runs through the night only on Fridays, Saturdays and the eve of public holidays. The Opera Ball falls on a Thursday. We confirm the exact drop-off and collection points with you before the day.

The Opera Ball officially ends at 05:00; the Kaffeesiederball at 04:30. Most guests leave between two and four in the morning. With a hotel room a few minutes away you sleep, have a late breakfast, perhaps an hour in a coffeehouse, and drive back to Prague in the afternoon, home in time for dinner.

### Plan two: back to Prague the same night

It can be done. Leave the ball at 01:00, after the midnight quadrille, and you are at your Prague door around 04:30, asleep for most of the motorway. For Vienna we suggest plan one. Three and a half hours each way plus a night on the dance floor is a long day, and the morning after a ball is part of the pleasure.

## The closer option: Dresden's SemperOpernball

If seven hours on the road feels like too much, Dresden holds its own opera ball one day after Vienna's. The **SemperOpernball** takes place on Friday, 5 February 2027 in the Semperoper, with an open-air ball on the Theaterplatz in front of the opera house on the same night.

Dresden is about 150 km from Prague, an hour and a half to two hours on the D8 and A17. Here the same-night return is the natural plan: leave Prague in the late afternoon already dressed, and be back in your own hotel bed before sunrise. Tickets are sold by the ball's organiser. ## Frequently asked questions

**When is the Vienna Opera Ball in 2027?**
Thursday, 4 February 2027, at the Vienna State Opera. Entry begins at 20:15, the opening ceremony at 22:00, and the ball officially ends at 05:00.

**When is the Vienna ball season in 2027?**
The season opens on 11 November 2026 and ends on Shrove Tuesday, 9 February 2027. Most of the famous balls fall between mid-January and early February.

**How long is the drive from Prague to Vienna?**
About 333 km and three and a half hours door to door, on motorway almost all the way. Allow a little more on Friday afternoons and in heavy winter weather.

**What time should we leave Prague on the day of the ball?**
By 11:00 at the latest. You arrive in Vienna around 14:30, which leaves time to check in, dress and eat before the doors open.

**Can the chauffeur wait and drive us back to Prague the same night?**
Yes, if it is arranged when you book. Leaving Vienna at 01:00 brings you to Prague at about 04:30. For Vienna we recommend staying the night; for Dresden the same-night return is easy.

**Can you get us tickets to the Opera Ball?**
No. Tickets are sold only by the Opera Ball Office of the Vienna State Opera, and other balls sell through their own organisers. We provide the car and chauffeur.

**Do we need passports between Prague and Vienna?**
Czechia, Austria and Germany are all in the Schengen area, so there is normally no border stop. Carry your passport or national ID, as police can make spot checks.

**How far ahead should we book the car?**
For the last week of January and the first week of February, at least two weeks ahead. Ball nights are fixed dates and the same evenings are requested by many guests.
