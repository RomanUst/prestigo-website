A **transfer to Prague Airport** is simple to plan once you know three things: how long before departure the airport wants you there, which terminal your flight leaves from, and how long the drive takes from your part of the city at that hour. Add them together, leave a small margin, and that is your pickup time. This guide does the sums for Schengen and non-Schengen flights, shows where a private car stops at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, and covers the extras that add time, such as a VAT refund or ski bags, using Václav Havel Airport's own 2026 guidance. - **Terminals:** Terminal 1 for flights to countries outside the Schengen area, Terminal 2 for Schengen destinations. They stand side by side, linked by a covered walkway.
- **Drop-off:** the Express zone directly in front of your terminal's departures entrance, with ten free minutes to unload.
- **Drive from the centre:** 25–40 minutes by day, under 25 minutes before 06:00, up to 55 minutes in the weekday rush hours.
- **The car:** Mercedes-Benz E-Class or S-Class for up to 3 passengers, V-Class for up to 6 passengers and 6 bags. One fixed quote agreed before pickup.

## When to leave your hotel: work backwards from the flight

**1. How early the airport wants you.** Prague Airport's standing advice is **at least two hours** before departure if you have bags to check. For the summer peak it raised that to **2.5 hours**, with **another half hour** for long-haul flights that pass through passport control. With hand luggage only and a boarding pass on your phone you can come later, but the airport still asks for ample time. Your airline's rules apply in every case: most counters open two hours before departure and close 40 minutes before.

**2. The drive.** Václav Havel Airport is about 20 km west of the centre.

| From | Off-peak | Weekday rush hour (07:30–09:30, 16:00–18:30) |
|---|---|---|
| Dejvice, Prague 6 | 15–25 min | 25–35 min |
| Vinohrady, Smíchov, Anděl | 25–35 min | 35–50 min |
| Old Town, Wenceslas Square, Malá Strana | 30–40 min | 45–55 min |
| Karlín, Holešovice, Žižkov | 30–40 min | 45–55 min |

In 2026 the airport warns of roadworks at the Aviatická junction, the last intersection before the terminals, which lengthen rush-hour journeys. Your chauffeur routes around it where possible, but for a weekday flight between 09:00 and 11:00 use the rush-hour column.

**3. A margin.** Ten to fifteen minutes covers loading the luggage, a slow hotel lift and the walk from the drop-off zone to the check-in desk.

Put together: for a **10:40 flight to London** (non-Schengen, Terminal 1) on a Tuesday, be at the airport by about 08:00 and leave an Old Town hotel at **07:10**. For a **14:15 flight to Amsterdam** (Schengen, Terminal 2) with checked bags, be there by 12:15 and leave Vinohrady at about **11:35**. Before dawn the roads are empty; our guide to [late-night arrivals and early-morning departures](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-airport-late-night-arrivals) covers those hours.

## Terminal 1 or Terminal 2? Where your chauffeur drops you

The rule is by destination, not by airline. **Terminal 1** serves every flight to a country outside the Schengen area: the United Kingdom and Ireland, the United States and Canada, the Gulf, India, East Asia and Türkiye. **Terminal 2** serves Schengen countries: Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Austria, Switzerland and the rest of the zone. The terminal is printed on your boarding pass; give us the flight number and your chauffeur confirms it on the day.

Private cars stop in the **Express zones**: **P1 Express** directly in front of Terminal 1 and **P2 Express** in front of Terminal 2 departures. The airport allows ten free minutes in each zone, enough to unload at the door; your chauffeur handles the barrier ticket. If you need help inside, with a pushchair or a lot of luggage, say so when you book and the chauffeur will park and walk you to the check-in desk. A late terminal change means a short walk along the covered walkway, not a missed flight.

## Inside the terminal: check-in, bag drop, security and FastTrack

**Terminal 2 (Schengen)** has one central security checkpoint behind the departures hall and no passport control. Its CT scanners let you leave laptops and liquids in your cabin bag. Self-service bag drop for participating airlines is at counters 272–295. Terminal 2 also has the airport's paid **Security FastTrack**: a separate entrance to the checkpoint, open daily from 04:00 to 21:00, bookable online at least two hours ahead or bought at a kiosk by the checkpoint for immediate use. Every traveller, including a child, needs their own pass, and it is only for standard cabin luggage, so it cannot be used with a pushchair.

**Terminal 1 (non-Schengen)** works the other way round: **passport control first**, then **security at the gate**, which opens shortly before boarding. There is no FastTrack lane, and the older rules apply at gate screening: electronics out of the bag, liquids in 100 ml containers in one resealable one-litre bag. Self-service bag drop is at counters 130–135. The passport queue is why the airport asks long-haul passengers to come half an hour earlier.

For a fully private departure, the airport's **Club Continental VIP service** checks you in, takes you through its own security channel and drives you to the aircraft; book it at least 48 hours ahead. ## Luggage, VAT refund and ski bags: what adds time

- **Oversize items.** Skis, snowboards, golf bags, prams, bicycles and large instruments go to a dedicated **oversize baggage counter** in both terminals after you report them at your normal check-in desk. Tell the airline when you book the flight, and tell us the number of ski bags so we send the right car; our [ski transfer guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-ski-transfers-2026) explains how ski kit changes the choice.
- **VAT refund for non-EU residents.** The kiosks that stamp the form and the Interchange office that pays out are in the **Terminal 1 departures hall** only. The goods must be available for inspection, so **do not check them in** before the stamp. If you fly from Terminal 2, walk to Terminal 1 first, which is why we suggest an extra 30 minutes. Purchases must exceed CZK 2,000 from one shop on one day.

## Early mornings, rush hour and peak weeks

Prague's first departure wave leaves around 06:00, and at 04:00 the drive from the centre takes 25 minutes or less. The difficult slots are **mid-morning weekday departures**, when the rush hour and the Aviatická roadworks coincide, and the **holiday peaks**: the week before Christmas, the days around New Year, Easter and the summer holidays. On those days add the airport's extra 30 minutes and book the car a few days ahead. On a snow morning the runways are cleared quickly, but the streets are slower; your chauffeur builds the extra minutes in, and if the airline delays the flight, we move the pickup rather than leave you waiting in the terminal.

## Frequently asked questions

**How early should I leave my hotel for a flight from Prague Airport?**
Add the airport's recommended arrival time (two hours with checked bags, 2.5 to 3 hours for a non-Schengen flight from Terminal 1) to the drive from your district (25 to 40 minutes by day, up to 55 in rush hour) and a 10–15 minute margin. From the Old Town that means about 2 h 45 min before a Schengen flight and 3 h 15 min before a non-Schengen one.

**Which terminal do I need at Prague Airport?**
Terminal 1 for flights to countries outside the Schengen area, including the UK, the US, the Gulf, India and Asia. Terminal 2 for Schengen countries such as Germany, France, Italy and Spain. The destination decides, not the airline, and the terminal is printed on your boarding pass.

**Where does a private car drop you at Prague Airport?**
In the Express zone directly in front of the departures entrance: P1 Express at Terminal 1 and P2 Express at Terminal 2. The airport allows ten free minutes for unloading, and your chauffeur handles the barrier ticket.

**Is there a fast-track security lane at Prague Airport?**
Yes, at Terminal 2 only. Security FastTrack is open daily from 04:00 to 21:00 and can be booked online at least two hours ahead or bought at a kiosk by the checkpoint. Each passenger, including children, needs their own pass, and it is only for standard cabin luggage. Terminal 1 has no fast-track lane; security there is at the gate, after passport control.

**Can I claim a VAT refund before a Terminal 2 flight?**
Yes, but the kiosks and the payout office are in the Terminal 1 departures hall only, and the goods must be shown unchecked. Go to Terminal 1 first, then return by the covered walkway to check in at Terminal 2. Allow an extra 30 minutes.

**What if my flight time changes after I book the transfer?**

**Can the chauffeur pick me up inside the Old Town?**
Many Old Town and Malá Strana streets are closed to through traffic, so hotels there use a regular pickup point a short walk away. We confirm the exact spot with your hotel the evening before, and your chauffeur helps with the luggage from the door.
