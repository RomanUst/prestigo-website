## Arriving from India: airport pickup at Terminal 1 or 2

Most journeys from India reach Prague with one connection — through a Gulf hub, Istanbul, or a European hub such as Frankfurt, Munich or Vienna. Which way you connect decides where you land:

- **Via Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi or Istanbul** — a non-Schengen flight. You arrive at **Terminal 1** and pass passport control in Prague. With a large family and a busy arrivals bank, this can take time.
- **Via Frankfurt, Munich, Vienna or another Schengen airport** — your passports were already checked at the first stop. You land at **Terminal 2** as a Schengen flight and walk straight to baggage reclaim.

Either way, the pickup works the same. We track the flight, so a late connection simply moves the chauffeur's time. After landing you have **60 minutes of free waiting** for immigration and the baggage belt; the chauffeur stands just past the customs exit with your name on a board and takes over the luggage trolleys from there. For a terminal-by-terminal walk-through, see our [Prague airport transfer service](https://rideprestigo.com/services/airport-transfer).

## A private car with driver for families from India: which car, how many

Luggage, not passengers, decides the car. Families travelling from India for two or three weeks usually carry one large suitcase per person, sometimes more, plus cabin bags. Our three cars are:

- **Mercedes-Benz E-Class or S-Class** — up to 3 passengers with 2 large suitcases and 2 cabin bags. Right for a couple, or parents with one child and light luggage.
- **Mercedes-Benz V-Class** — up to 6 passengers with 6 large suitcases and 6 cabin bags. The usual choice for a family of four to six. The sliding side doors and high seats make it easy for older travellers to get in and out.

## Prague by the hour: a city day at the grandparents' pace

Prague's historic centre is beautiful and tiring: cobbles, hills, long walks between sights. A car with driver by the hour lets three generations see the same city without anyone being left behind. The chauffeur drops you at one entrance, waits, and collects you at the next.

A typical family day is four to six hours: the Castle district from the top so the walk is downhill, a lunch stop chosen by you, the Old Town and the riverside in the afternoon. Some family members can rest in the car or return to the hotel while others continue — the car simply does one extra drop-off.

One clarification that matters: your chauffeur is a driver, not a licensed tour guide. If you want a guided tour of the Castle or the Jewish Quarter, book a guide separately and we time the pickups around the tour. For how hourly hire works — the 2-hour minimum, extensions on the day, sample plans — see our [car with driver by the hour guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/car-with-driver-prague-by-the-hour).

## Day trips without a coach tour

A coach tour runs on a fixed timetable for forty people; a family with grandparents and children rarely fits that rhythm. A private day trip leaves when you are ready, stops when someone needs a break, and returns when the children are tired. Three that work well for families:

- **Kutná Hora** — about 70 km and an hour from Prague: St Barbara's Cathedral and the Sedlec Ossuary. An easy half day. See the [Prague–Kutná Hora route](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/prague-kutna-hora).
- **Karlovy Vary** — about 130 km and 1.5 hours west: the spa colonnades, ideal for a slow walk with older parents.
- **Český Krumlov** — about 175 km and 2.5 hours south: a castle above a river bend and a medieval town. A full, rewarding day.

Our guide to [private day trips from Prague](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/private-day-trips-from-prague) covers each destination, how long you need there, and what to see.

## Practical notes for travellers from India

- **One Schengen visa covers the trip.** Czechia, Austria, Slovakia and Hungary are all in the Schengen Area. Apply through the country where you will spend the most nights.
- **Vegetarian food.** Prague has many vegetarian and Indian restaurants, and Czech menus often list meatless dishes as *bezmasá jídla*. Tell us restaurants you have chosen and we plan the stops around them; on day trips, it helps to carry snacks for the children.
- **Stay in touch.** We confirm details by email and stay reachable on WhatsApp, so relatives organising the trip from India can follow the plan too.
- **Book ahead in peak months.** Summer and the Christmas-market season fill up early, especially V-Class vans.
