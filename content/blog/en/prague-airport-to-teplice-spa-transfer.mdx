There is no direct train or bus from the airport, so a pre-booked car is the only way to reach the spa without changing in Prague. Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall, loads the luggage and any mobility equipment, and stops at the entrance of your spa house. ## The road from Prague Airport to Teplice: 70 minutes on the D8

Václav Havel Airport lies on the north-western edge of Prague, which suits this journey. The car goes around the north of the city to the D8, the motorway that links Prague with Dresden, and does not pass through the centre. From there the road runs north across the Elbe plain and through the České středohoří, a range of low volcanic hills. The motorway has been complete along its whole length since December 2016. The car leaves it near Řehlovice and follows Road 63 for the last ten kilometres into Teplice.

The whole journey is inside the Czech Republic, so there is no border and no document check on the way. Roadworks appear on the D8 near Lovosice from time to time. The chauffeur checks the route before departure and takes the parallel road if a section is slow. On Friday afternoons, allow 10 to 15 minutes more leaving the Prague area.

## After a long flight: what happens in the arrivals hall

Flights from the Gulf, Central Asia, the United Kingdom and other countries outside Schengen arrive at Terminal 1, with passport control before baggage reclaim. Schengen flights use Terminal 2. Give us the flight number when you book. It tells the chauffeur the terminal and the real landing time.

The chauffeur waits just past the customs exit with your family name on a board. The free hour of waiting begins when the aircraft lands. It covers a slow passport queue, a wheelchair that arrives at the oversized-baggage belt after the suitcases, and a child who needs a few minutes before the next leg.

If someone in the family uses the airport's assistance service for passengers with reduced mobility, request it through your airline at least 48 hours before the flight. The airport's staff then bring the passenger from the aircraft door to the arrivals hall, and the chauffeur takes over from there. ## Which spa house? Give us the name, not only the town

Teplice is a working town of about 50,000 people, and the spa houses are spread around the parks near its centre. Lázně Teplice, the main spa company, runs several of them, including the **Beethoven**, **Imperial** (Císařské lázně) and **Stone** (Kamenné lázně) spa houses. Each has its own entrance and reception. The Stone Spa House treats adults and children from 18 months, and the children's programmes there are the reason many families make this journey.

"Teplice spa" is not enough for a booking. Send us the name of the house written on your treatment confirmation, and the chauffeur drives to that entrance. The spa company allows short-term standing in front of the spa houses, which is enough time to unload cases, set up a wheelchair and walk with you to reception.

One more practical point: the spa asks that a child patient is accompanied by an adult for the whole stay, and the children's programmes usually run for four to six weeks. Pack and choose the car for a stay of that length.

## Wheelchairs, walking frames and child seats: choosing the car

Passengers and equipment decide the car on this route.

- **E-Class or S-Class:** one patient with one companion, or a couple, with ordinary luggage. The S-Class has more legroom and a quieter rear cabin, which helps after an overnight flight.
- **V-Class:** families, and anyone travelling with mobility equipment. It seats six with six large bags. The sliding doors open wide, and the seats are higher than in a saloon, so getting in and out takes less effort. A folded wheelchair, a walking frame or a child's therapy stroller travels in the luggage space.

The V-Class is a passenger van, not a ramp vehicle. The passenger moves from the wheelchair to a seat, with the chauffeur's help if you wish, and the chair travels folded. If the chair does not fold, or the passenger cannot leave it, tell us before you book so that we can say honestly whether we can help.

Child seats are free: a rear-facing infant seat, a toddler seat or a booster. Give us the child's age and weight. For a child who needs a special postural seat, bring your own and we will fit it.

Families of seven or more, or with luggage for six weeks, travel in two cars that leave together. ## Train and bus from the airport: what the alternative involves

No public transport runs from Prague Airport to Teplice. You take a bus or a taxi into Prague, reach a railway or coach station and continue north from there. The stations in Teplice are about 1 km from the spa buildings, a 15-minute walk or a ride on a local bus.

For one traveller with a small bag, that is manageable. It is hard with a child after surgery, an elderly parent or several weeks of luggage: two or three changes, stairs and platforms, and nobody to carry the cases. A private car turns the same journey into one seat from the arrivals hall to reception.

## Relatives who arrive and leave: several airport runs in one stay

A stay of four to six weeks rarely means one arrival and one departure. One parent flies home after the first week and another relative arrives to take over. Grandparents visit for a few days. Sometimes the doctors extend the programme.

Tell us the whole plan when you book and we put every transfer into one confirmation. Each transfer has its own flight number, so each is tracked separately. If the spa changes your dates, send one message and we move the car.

For the flight home, count backwards from departure. Prague Airport advises arriving at least two hours before a Schengen flight and two and a half to three hours before a non-Schengen one. With the drive and a margin:

| Flight | Leave Teplice |
|---|---|
| **Schengen (Terminal 2)** | about 3.5 hours before departure |
| **Non-Schengen (Terminal 1)** | 4 to 4.5 hours before departure |

## Free days: Prague and Dresden are both about an hour away

Treatment fills the mornings, and the weekends are usually free. From Teplice two cities are close enough for an easy day.

- **Prague** is about 90 km and a little over an hour away. The car waits while you see the city, or you add [hourly hire](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/car-with-driver-prague-by-the-hour) for shopping and dinner.

## Frequently asked questions

**How far is Teplice from Prague Airport?**
About 95 km. A private car takes roughly 1 hour 10 minutes, mostly on the D8 motorway, and does not go through the centre of Prague.

**Is there a train or bus from Prague Airport to Teplice?**
Not a direct one. You first travel into Prague and continue by train or coach. The stations in Teplice are about 1 km from the spa buildings.

**Where does the chauffeur meet us?**
In the public arrivals hall, just past the customs exit, with your name on a board: Terminal 1 for non-Schengen flights and Terminal 2 for Schengen flights.

**Can you take a passenger who uses a wheelchair?**
Yes, if the passenger can move to a seat and the wheelchair folds. It travels in the luggage space of the V-Class. Our cars have no ramp or lift, so tell us about the chair when you book.

**Do you bring child seats?**
Yes, at no charge. Tell us the age and weight of each child. You can also bring a special seat of your own and we will fit it.

**Does the car stop at the entrance of the spa house?**
Yes. Give us the name of the house, for example Beethoven, Imperial or Stone Spa House, and the chauffeur drives to that entrance and helps with the luggage to reception.

**When should we leave Teplice for a flight from Prague?**
About three and a half hours before a Schengen flight and four to four and a half hours before a non-Schengen flight. Allow more time if you need airport assistance.

**Can we book the arrival, the return and a relative's flight together?**
Yes. All transfers go into one confirmation with one fixed quote, and each date can be changed if the treatment programme changes.
