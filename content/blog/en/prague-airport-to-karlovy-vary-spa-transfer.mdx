You do not go into Prague at all. Mariánské Lázně is about two hours from the airport and Františkovy Lázně about two and a half. ## From the arrivals hall to the car

Give us the flight number when you book, not only the airline and the time. The flight number tells the chauffeur which terminal you will use and when you really land. Flights from the Gulf, the United Kingdom, the United States, Turkey and other countries outside Schengen arrive at Terminal 1, where passport control comes before baggage reclaim. Flights from within Schengen arrive at Terminal 2 with no passport check.

The chauffeur stands just past the customs exit with your name on a board, takes the luggage trolley and walks you to the car. The free hour of waiting starts at landing, so a slow baggage belt costs you nothing. If you would like a more formal reception, our [meet and greet guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-airport-meet-and-greet) explains the airport's own fast-track and lounge services.

Two things are worth doing before you fly:

- **Tell the hotel when you will arrive.** A flight that lands at 22:00 puts you at a Karlovy Vary reception desk after midnight. Most spa hotels have a night porter, but smaller houses ask you to announce a late arrival.
- **Send us a phone number that works on landing**, or a messaging app you will open on the airport Wi-Fi.

## Prague Airport to Karlovy Vary: 1.5 hours on the D6

Václav Havel Airport sits on the western edge of Prague, and the D6 to Karlovy Vary starts almost at its gate. A transfer from the city centre adds 20 to 30 minutes of urban traffic before it reaches the same motorway; from the terminal you are on open road within minutes. The route climbs across the Bohemian plateau past Nové Strašecí and Krušovice and drops into the spa valley. It stays inside the Czech Republic, so there is no border and no document check.

The D6 is being widened to full motorway standard, and the last sections towards Karlovy Vary are scheduled for completion through 2027. Your chauffeur checks each section before departure and uses the older Route 6 if a construction zone is slow. On Friday afternoons in summer, allow 10 to 15 minutes more.

If the day allows, one stop is worth making: **Loket**, a small medieval town with a castle in a bend of the Ohře river, ten minutes before Karlovy Vary. ## Mariánské Lázně and Františkovy Lázně from the airport

For the two western spa towns the usual route is different. The car takes the Prague ring road to the D5, passes Plzeň on its bypass and leaves the motorway at exit 128 for Highway 21, which runs north through the hills into the spa triangle.

- **Mariánské Lázně:** about 165 km and two hours. See [Prague to Mariánské Lázně](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/prague-marianske-lazne).
- **Františkovy Lázně:** about 175 km and two and a half hours, continuing past Cheb. The German border is a few minutes beyond the town, but the journey itself is domestic. See [Prague to Františkovy Lázně](https://rideprestigo.com/routes/prague-frantiskovy-lazne).

On Friday afternoons the D5 out of Prague can add 15 to 20 minutes. There is no direct bus or train from the airport to either town; public transport means going into Prague first. All three towns have been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 2021 as part of the Great Spa Towns of Europe.

## The last 500 metres: spa-zone rules at the hotel door

The centres of the spa towns are closed to ordinary traffic, and this is where a transfer most often goes wrong.

**Karlovy Vary.** The spa centre around the Mill Colonnade and the hot spring is traffic-restricted, with regulated drop-off points and controlled access. Hotels inside the zone arrange entry for their guests' cars. Book with the **name of the hotel**, not only the street, so that we can agree the approach with reception before you land.

**Mariánské Lázně.** The town's spa zone covers Goethovo náměstí and the streets around the colonnade, and vehicles need a municipal entry permit. Under the town's published rules, guests staying in a hotel inside the zone do not need the permit on the first and the last day of their stay. Those are exactly the days of your transfer, so the car can drive to the entrance.

In both towns the chauffeur carries the cases into the lobby.

## Luggage for two or three weeks: choosing the car

A spa cure usually lasts one to three weeks, and the luggage is planned for the stay, not for the flight. Count the cases before you choose.

- **E-Class:** a couple with up to three large cases.
- **S-Class:** the same capacity, with an executive rear cabin for the long motorway stretch after an overnight flight.
- **V-Class:** families and anyone with more than three large cases. It seats six with six large bags, the sliding doors and higher seats are easier for older passengers, and a folded wheelchair or walking frame fits in the luggage space. Mention it when you book.

## The return: timing the drive back to your flight

Book the return at the same time as the arrival. Both legs go into one quote, and if the hotel extends your programme you change the date with one message.

Work backwards from the departure time. Prague Airport advises arriving at least two hours before a flight with checked bags, and two and a half to three hours before a non-Schengen flight from Terminal 1. Add the drive and a short margin:

| Leaving from | Schengen flight (Terminal 2) | Non-Schengen flight (Terminal 1) |
|---|---|---|
| **Karlovy Vary** | leave about 4 hours before departure | 4.5 to 5 hours before |
| **Mariánské Lázně** | about 4.5 hours before | 5 to 5.5 hours before |
| **Františkovy Lázně** | about 5 hours before | 5.5 to 6 hours before |

The D6 from Karlovy Vary ends close to the airport, so the last minutes of the drive are not city traffic. In winter the uplands on both routes can have snow or fog while Prague is clear, and we move the pickup earlier on those days. ## A car during your stay

A spa programme leaves free afternoons, and most guests want one or two days out.

- **The other spa towns.** Františkovy Lázně is 40 km from Mariánské Lázně and 50 km from Karlovy Vary, so the whole triangle fits into one day with a car.
- **Loket** is ten minutes from Karlovy Vary and fills a half day with its castle and lunch by the river.
- **Prague for a day.** Leave after breakfast, spend the day in the city with the car waiting, and return for dinner. A same-day return can be extended with [hourly hire](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/car-with-driver-prague-by-the-hour) in Prague.

Arrange these when you book the airport transfer, or from the hotel a day or two ahead.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is the drive from Prague Airport to Karlovy Vary?**
About 1.5 hours for roughly 120 km. The D6 motorway starts next to the airport, so the car does not pass through Prague.

**How far is Mariánské Lázně from Prague Airport?**
About 165 km, or two hours by private car, on the D5 past Plzeň and then Highway 21. Františkovy Lázně is about 175 km and two and a half hours.

**Where does the chauffeur meet us at Prague Airport?**
In the public arrivals hall, just past the customs exit, with your name on a board: Terminal 1 for flights from outside Schengen, Terminal 2 for Schengen flights.

**What happens if the flight is delayed?**
The chauffeur tracks the flight and comes for the real landing time. Sixty minutes of free waiting after landing cover passport control and baggage.

**Can the car drive to the door of a hotel in the spa zone?**
Yes. In Karlovy Vary the hotel arranges access to the restricted centre for guests' cars. In Mariánské Lázně hotel guests need no entry permit on the first and last day of their stay. Give us the hotel name when you book.

**How much luggage fits in the car?**
An E-Class or S-Class takes up to three passengers and three large cases. A V-Class takes six passengers and six large bags, with room for a folded wheelchair if you tell us in advance.

**When should we leave Karlovy Vary for a flight from Prague?**
About four hours before a Schengen flight and four and a half to five hours before a non-Schengen one. Add half an hour from Mariánské Lázně and an hour from Františkovy Lázně.

**Can we book the arrival and the return together?**
Yes, and it is the simplest way. Both transfers are confirmed in one fixed quote, and the return date can be changed if your stay is extended.
