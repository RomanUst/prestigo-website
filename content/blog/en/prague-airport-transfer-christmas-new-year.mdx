A **Prague Airport transfer at Christmas** needs more planning than the same ride in November, for one reason: between 24 December and 1 January the city runs on four public holidays, and on Christmas Eve daytime public transport stops at about 18:00. Flights keep landing, but trams thin out, large shops shut and fewer cars are on the road for hire. This guide goes through the holiday period day by day, from 20 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, so you know what to expect at the hour you land or leave. ## Why the holiday week is different in Prague

**Czech Christmas happens on the evening of 24 December.** Families sit down to dinner in the early evening, and the city empties. A walk-up ride that takes two minutes to find on an ordinary Thursday can take much longer at 19:00 on Christmas Eve.

**Public transport switches to night mode early.** Last winter, daytime trams and buses on 24 December finished at about 18:00 and the night network took over until about 07:00 the next morning, with the metro running every 20 minutes until around midnight. For the airport, that leaves the night buses 907 and 910: roughly 45 minutes to the centre, then a change or a walk at the other end.

**Flights bunch together.** Arrivals usually peak just before Christmas and again before New Year, and a full terminal means slower baggage belts and a longer queue at the taxi rank.

## Day by day: 20 December to 3 January

### 20–23 December: the arrival rush

Ordinary working days with extra traffic. The drive to the centre takes 25–35 minutes off-peak and up to 55 minutes in the weekday rush, roughly 16:00–18:30. If your hotel is in the pedestrian part of the Old Town, the car stops at the nearest permitted point and the chauffeur helps with the bags from there.

### 24 December: Christmas Eve

Morning and midday arrivals are easy: roads are clear and shops are open until noon. If you land **after 17:00**, treat it as a late-night arrival even though it is early evening: daytime lines are finishing, restaurants are closing and the roads are almost empty, so the drive is often under 30 minutes. Our guide to [late-night arrivals at Prague Airport](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-airport-late-night-arrivals) explains how the night buses work if you need them.

### 25 and 26 December

Both days are public holidays on Sunday timetables. Last winter, daytime trams and buses started at about 07:00 on 25 December, and the airport trolleybus 59 started with the metro. Large shops stay closed, but the main markets stay open and shops at the airport and the main railway station are exempt from the closure law. These are the calmest days of the year on the road, and a good time for a transfer straight from the airport to the mountains; see our [Prague ski transfers guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-ski-transfers-2026).

### 31 December: New Year's Eve

Flights land all day, but the centre closes in stages: in recent years there has been no parking on Wenceslas Square from midday, side streets have closed from late afternoon and the approaches to Old Town Square in the evening. If you can choose, land by early afternoon. If you cannot, give us the hotel address when you book, and your chauffeur will plan the closest legal drop-off and walk you to the door. The night itself is covered in our [New Year's Eve in Prague guide](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/new-years-eve-prague-2026).

### 1 January: New Year's Day

Last winter the metro ran until just after 02:00, then night lines until about 07:00. A flight before 09:00 therefore means leaving the hotel while only the night lines are running, after a short night, and while streets near Wenceslas Square may still be closed. Reserve this transfer before you travel and agree the exact pickup point. The same applies to 2–3 January, the weekend when most visitors fly home: book the return together with the arrival.

## What to arrange before you land on a public holiday

- **Dinner on 24 December.** Many restaurants close for the evening. Hotel restaurants and some places in the centre open with a set menu and advance booking only. Reserve before you fly.
- **Food and essentials.** Shops with a sales area over 200 m² close at 12:00 on the 24th and stay closed on the 25th, the 26th and 1 January. Small shops, pharmacies and the shops at the airport can open, and bottled water is in every car.
- **Your hotel's reception hours.** Smaller guesthouses and apartments sometimes run reduced hours over Christmas, so send them your arrival time and save the door code offline.

For the wider picture of the month, with weather, market dates and museum closures, see [Prague in December 2026](https://rideprestigo.com/blog/prague-in-december-2026).

## Flying out over the holidays: pickup times that work

Prague Airport asks passengers with checked bags to arrive at least two hours before departure, and in peak periods it raises that to two and a half hours, with a further half hour for long-haul flights through passport control. Christmas and New Year count as peak.

| Your flight | Be at the airport | Leave a hotel in the centre |
|---|---|---|
| Schengen, Terminal 2, checked bags | 2 h 30 min before | about 3 h 15 min before |
| Non-Schengen, Terminal 1 | 3 h before | about 3 h 45 min before |

Two examples. For a **10:15 flight to Dubai on 27 December** (Terminal 1), be at the airport by 07:15 and leave an Old Town hotel at about **06:30**. For a **07:00 flight to London on 1 January** (Terminal 1), aim to be at the airport by 04:00 and leave at about **03:25**, from a pickup point your chauffeur has checked against the night's closures.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Prague Airport transfers run on Christmas Day?**
Yes. The airport operates every day of the year and pre-booked private transfers run on 24, 25 and 26 December and on 1 January. Reserve in advance, because few cars are available at short notice on those days.

**Is there public transport from Prague Airport on Christmas Eve?**
Yes, but reduced. In the 2025/26 holiday timetable, daytime trams and buses ended at about 18:00 on 24 December, night lines ran from then until about 07:00 and the metro ran every 20 minutes until around midnight.

**How long does the drive from Prague Airport to the centre take over the holidays?**
About 25–35 minutes in normal traffic, often under 30 minutes on 24–26 December, and up to 55 minutes in the evening rush on 20–23 December.

**Can a car reach my hotel in the centre on New Year's Eve?**
During the day, usually yes. From late afternoon, streets around Wenceslas Square and Old Town Square close in stages, so the car stops at the nearest permitted point and the chauffeur walks you to the door.

**What time should I leave for an early flight on 1 January?**
Work back from the airport's peak advice: two and a half hours before a Schengen flight, three hours before a non-Schengen one, plus the drive. For a 07:00 flight to London, that is a pickup at about 03:25.

**How early should I book a Prague Airport transfer for Christmas or New Year?**
As soon as your flights are confirmed, ideally by late November for 24–26 December, 31 December and 1 January.
