## The route in brief

Karlovy Vary sits 130 kilometres west of Prague along the D6. By car it is a one-hour-forty-five drive on a good day — one road, no changes, door to door. By public transport it is a different proposition entirely, and the gap is wider than the map suggests.

## Chauffeur, bus, or train — honestly

**Train.** Avoid it for this route. There is no fast direct line; the journey runs three hours or more with a change, and the station sits a tram ride from the spa colonnade. For a spa town, it is the slowest option on the board.

**Bus.** The honest budget choice. RegioJet and FlixBus run frequent coaches from Prague's Florenc terminal in around two hours fifteen for a few hundred koruna. Comfortable, reliable, and the right answer if you are travelling light and your schedule is flexible.

**Private chauffeur.** Roughly one hour forty-five, collected from your hotel door and delivered to the colonnade or your spa hotel's entrance. One fixed price for the car, not per seat — which is why it makes most sense for two to four people, or anyone carrying luggage to a multi-night spa stay.

## When the drive is worth it

The private transfer earns its place in three situations. The first is a group: split between three or four passengers, the per-person cost lands close to two bus tickets, with none of the terminal-to-terminal friction. The second is luggage — spa stays mean larger cases, and a coach hold is not where you want a week's wardrobe. The third is time of day: an early checkout or a late spa dinner leaves you outside the bus timetable, and a chauffeur simply runs to your clock.

## What the drive itself is like

The D6 west out of Prague opens into Bohemian countryside within twenty minutes — rolling fields, the occasional castle on a ridge, and the spa hills rising as you approach. In a quiet Mercedes with the right driver it passes as part of the trip rather than a transfer between two points. Many guests ask for a short stop at the Loket castle viewpoint on the way; with a private car, that is a question, not a rebooking.
