|----------|-------------|
| operator edit to the payment engine | an edited amount becomes the next hold or charge |
| operator click to the legacy payment-link route | a link charges amount_eur, the full line amount |
| Stripe events of a group payment link to quote bookings | a payment made outside the quote |
