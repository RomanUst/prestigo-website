<objective>
An airport pickup is timed after landing on the server: the checkout takes the landing time and an offset, derives and validates the pickup in Prague time (DST and midnight safe), and stores both times on the booking.

Tracer: a card checkout from the airport with landing 2027-06-10 14:00 and offset 30 stores landing_date, landing_time '14:00', pickup_offset_min 30 and pickup_time '14:30'.

Purpose: STOP-05 server side (D-07, D-18). The wizard (91-10), admin (91-12), driver page (91-13), emails (91-14) and the move-pickup action (91-15) use lib/airport-pickup.ts and the stored columns.
Output: one pure module, validation and meta in compute.ts, row mapping in lib/supabase.ts, tests.
</objective>
