<objective>
Fix the confirmed, reproducible EN-leak bug on the 10 `force-static` pages that silently serve English on every non-EN locale, and wire them onto `getAlternates()`. This is the literal defect SEO-02 exists to eliminate — shipping "localized metadata" while 10 pages render EN on `/ru`, `/es`, `/fr`, `/ar`, `/hi`, `/zh` is not partial success (RESEARCH Pitfall 1, documented in 73-RTL-QA.md / deferred-items.md / WINDOWS #7).

Purpose: on `dynamic = 'force-static'` pages a request-time locale accessor resolves to the default locale (`en`) at static-generation time regardless of the URL being built. The next-intl-documented fix is to forward `{ locale } = await params` explicitly into both `generateMetadata({ params })` and the page body.

Output: 10 pages rendering genuine per-locale content; the noindex corporate-accounts D-06 violation closed; a new SEO-02 unit test.
</objective>
