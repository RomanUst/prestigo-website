1. conversationId from parseHookTag; hooksStart() null -> skip 'hooks_off'.
2. chat.conversation(id) already recorded with a final outcome and (for lead outcomes) an espo lead id -> complete sent (idempotent replay).
3. readConversation -> null -> skip 'conversation_missing'; createdAtSec * 1000 < hooksStart -> skip 'before_start'.
4. readMessages (maxPages 1) -> firstIncoming none -> skip 'no_incoming_message'. Contact = readContact(conversation.contact.id) (fallback the meta sender).
5. hasNoLeadLabel(labels) -> record 'no_lead_label', skip 'no_lead_label'. nonTargetReason({ senderEmail: message sender email or contact email, autoReply }) -> record 'filtered', skip 'filtered_<reason>'.
6. email = normalised contact email; phone = phoneUsable(contact phone).
7. byChatwootContact(contact.id): a customer row -> record 'customer', skip 'customer'; 2 rows -> treat as customer (ambiguous, no Lead).
8. email present: chatLookup(email): erasing -> skip 'identity_erasing'; customer -> record 'customer' (espo contact id kept), skip 'customer'. No identity: findContactIdByEmail -> found -> record 'known_contact' with that contact id, skip 'known_contact'. Then resolveChat(email, phone): erasing -> skip; customer (merged by phone) -> record 'customer', skip. Ensure the Contact: identity espo_contact_id, else findContactIdByEmail, else createChatContact with splitName(cleanContactName(name, email), email), email and phone; setEspoContact when it changed; setChatwootContact when the identity has none.
9. no email, usable phone: findContactIdsByPhone: exactly 1 -> link it; 0 -> createChatContact keyed on the phone; 2 -> no Contact. No email, no phone: no Contact (D-04).
10. D-20 (email present): findRecentOpenLeadByEmail(email, now - 7 days) -> found -> postNote(Lead, formatAppendNote(..., openMarker)) unless streamHasMarker -> record 'lead_adopted' with that lead id -> complete sent.
11. reserveLead({ personKey: personKeyFor(...), identityId, chatwootContactId: contact.id, phone: email ? null : phone, leadRef: chatLeadRef(aggregateUuid('conversation', id)) }).

    - not own: status = readLeadStatus(espoLeadId) (or findLeadByRef(leadRef)); missing or not isOpenLeadStatus -> closeLead(chatLeadId, 'closed_in_crm') and reserve again (now own -> create). Open -> postNote(Lead, append) unless streamHasMarker; when a Contact is known and the lead has none, updateLead cExistingContactId + setLeadEspo -> record 'lead_appended'.
12. complete 'sent'. Log `[chat-intake] key=<key> outcome=<outcome> code=<code>`.
