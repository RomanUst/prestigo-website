## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-83-38 | Spoofing | forged EspoCRM batch | high | mitigate | `Signature` HMAC over raw bytes, optional webhook-id pin, legacy header refused, fail-closed without secret (route tests) |
| T-83-39 | Tampering | replay of a captured EspoCRM batch (no timestamp in the protocol) | medium | mitigate | D-19 compensation: content-hash dedup 30 days, handlers re-read state (a replay changes nothing), narrow Contact.update surface, skipOwn |
| T-83-40 | Denial of Service | EspoCRM webhook deleted by a gone status / retry storms | medium | mitigate | gone status never used (test asserts no 410 in any case); 503 only for store failure; 200 for every business outcome |

| T-83-42 | Tampering | blanking Chatwoot data with empty CRM values | low | mitigate | empty or invalid values never pushed (Task 3 tests) |
