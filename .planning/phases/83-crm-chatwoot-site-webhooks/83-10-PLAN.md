<objective>
Handle the two operator actions in Chatwoot that matter to the CRM: adding an email/phone to a contact (D-12: find or create the EspoCRM Contact, link the open chat Lead, register the identity) and labelling a conversation `no-lead` (D-07: the chat Lead goes to Dead). Stamp the HOOK-04 origin fingerprint on every site write to a Chatwoot contact so the site's own writes are recognised when they come back.

Purpose: HOOK-01 (Contact created/updated once contact data appears) and HOOK-04 (origin tagging on outbound writes — success criterion 4's Chatwoot half).
Output: `lib/chat-contact.ts`, the two branches in `lib/hook-dispatch.ts`, fingerprint stamping in `lib/chatwoot-sync.ts`, tests, VPS-guard allowlist entry.
</objective>
