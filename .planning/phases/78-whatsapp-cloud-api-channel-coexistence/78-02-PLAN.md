Hook facts: INFRA_SECRET_KEY_NAMES = every NAME= in infra/vps/env/*.env.example whose name contains PASSWORD, SECRET, _KEY or TOKEN; a hit needs a value of at least 4 characters from [A-Za-z0-9+/_.:=-].
