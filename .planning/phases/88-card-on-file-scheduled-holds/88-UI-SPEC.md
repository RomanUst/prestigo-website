# Phase 88 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Card on File & Scheduled Holds. Four surfaces: (1) the public checkout payment step for card-on-file, (2) the admin booking payment panel with state badge, append-only ledger and actions, (3) the admin settings card for the card flow, (4) the four client payment emails in 7 locales. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker. Locked inputs: `88-CONTEXT.md` D-01..D-24 (cited as D-nn), `88-RESEARCH.md` (Patterns 9, 12, 13), the Phase 86/87 admin theme tokens (`86-UI-SPEC.md`, `87-UI-SPEC.md`, reused not reinvented) and the existing public checkout (`Step6Payment.tsx`) and email (`lib/email.ts`) look.
>
> **DRAFT LEGAL COPY.** Every string that authorises a hold or charge (consent label, "how payment works" panel, authorisation sentences in emails) is draft wording pending the Phase 89 legal review (D-12). The consent text version constant `CARD_CONSENT_VERSION` is stored per booking so the wording can change without breaking old records. Do not treat this copy as final legal text.
>
> **Prices.** No amount is typed anywhere in this contract. All money in `app/`, `components/`, `lib/payments/emails` is rendered from server values through `formatEUR` / `formatCZK` (`lib/currency.ts`) and ICU `{amount}` parameters. Example amounts below are written as `{amount}`.

## Surface 1 — Checkout payment step (public, 7 locales)

### Gate: `Step6Checkout` (D-09, Research Pattern 9)

`BookingWizard` renders `Step6Checkout` where it renders `Step6Payment` today. The gate calls `POST /api/card-checkout/init` once when the step mounts and renders one of:

| Result | Renders |
|--------|---------|
| In flight | The existing `Booking.step6.loadingPayment` line ("Loading payment..."), 14px `--warmgrey`, inside the same `max-w-[560px]` column, with the booking summary block already visible above it so the page does not jump. |
| `eligible: false` (flag off, email not on the canary allowlist, trip type `round_trip` or `daily`, D-19) | Legacy `Step6Payment`, byte-for-byte unchanged. A client who is not eligible must never see any trace of the card flow. |

| Init request fails (network, 5xx, 429) | **Fail open to legacy `Step6Payment`** (Claude's choice): checkout must never be blocked by the new flow, and `off` equals today's behaviour. No error is shown for the failed init. |

### "How payment works" panel (`CardPaymentExplainer`) — D-11, D-12

Surface `--anthracite-mid`, 1px `--anthracite-light` border, radius 4px, padding 24px, margin-bottom 32px. A 3px `--copper` rule runs down the leading edge (left in LTR, right in RTL via `border-inline-start`).

- **Headline** (Heading 20/400 line-height 1.2, `--offwhite`): setup variant "Nothing is charged now."; held-now variant "The amount is held now and charged after your trip."
- **Rows**, 16px apart, each a two-line stack: caption `.label` style (11px, uppercase, `--copper-light`) then body 14px/300 `--offwhite` line-height 1.5. In `ar` and `hi` the caption letter-spacing is reset to `normal` and uppercase is dropped (existing `:lang()` rule in `globals.css`; use the `.label` class so it applies).

| Variant | Row | Caption | Body (EN draft) |
|---------|-----|---------|-----------------|
| setup | 1 | "Today" | "We save your card securely with Stripe. Your bank may ask you to confirm it." |
| setup | 2 | `{holdDate}` (the exact date, see below) | "We hold {amount} on your card. A hold is not a charge, but your bank may show it as pending." |
| setup | 3 | "After your trip" | "The hold becomes the final charge for your trip. If the price changes, only the agreed amount is taken." |
| held now | 1 | "Today" | "We hold {amount} on your card. A hold is not a charge, but your bank may show it as pending." |
| held now | 2 | "After your trip" | "The held amount is charged. If the final price is lower, only the lower amount is taken and the rest is released." |

- **Hold date (D-12).** `{holdDate}` is the Prague calendar date of `hold_at` returned by `/init`, formatted `formatLocaleDate(iso, locale)` (long date with year, Latin digits in `ar`/`hi`/`zh`), e.g. "12 November 2026". It is always the **exact date**; the contract never says "about" or "shortly after" (D-12, owner re-confirmed in D-21). The same string feeds the row caption, the consent text and the card-saved email so the three can never disagree.
- Amount is the server-confirmed amount in the selected currency (`formatEUR` / `formatCZK`); it updates when promo or currency changes.
- The whole panel is `aria-live="off"`; it is read as ordinary content. Do not use `role="alert"`.

### Consent checkbox (HOLD-01)

- Native `<input type="checkbox">`, **unticked on every mount and every remount**, never prefilled, never written to the Zustand store or sessionStorage, never remembered across reloads.
- Visual box 20x20, `accent-color: var(--copper)`; the whole row (checkbox + label) is one `<label>` with `min-height: 44px`, `padding: 8px 0`, `display: flex`, `gap: 16px`, so the tap target is the full row. Focus ring: 2px `--copper` outline, 2px offset (same as the legacy input focus).
- Label text 14px/300, `--offwhite`, line-height 1.5 (this is the consent text, hashed and stored). EN drafts, one per variant:
  - setup: "I authorise Prestigo to save this card and to hold {amount} on it on {holdDate}, then charge it after my trip is completed. If the agreed price of my booking changes, I authorise Prestigo to charge or release the difference."
  - held now: "I authorise Prestigo to hold {amount} on this card now, to save this card for this purpose, and to charge it after my trip is completed. If the agreed price of my booking changes, I authorise Prestigo to charge or release the difference."
- **Invalidation rule.** Any change that alters the consent text (amount, currency, mode, hold date) clears the tick and announces, in the polite live region, "The payment details changed. Please review and tick the box again." The server also rejects `consent !== true` with 422 `CONSENT_REQUIRED`.
- Error state (only from a 422 `CONSENT_REQUIRED`, which the disabled button normally prevents): the row gets a 1px `#f87171` border, radius 4px, and the message below with `role="alert"`; focus moves to the checkbox.

### Submit button

`className="btn-primary"`, `width: 100%`, `minHeight: 44px`, same disabled style as legacy (`opacity: 0.4`, `cursor: not-allowed`, `aria-disabled`). `aria-describedby` points at the consent label.

| State | Label (EN draft) | Enabled |
|-------|------------------|---------|
| Elements not ready and no load error | "Loading..." (existing `Booking.step6.loading`) | no |
| Ready, consent unticked | setup: "Save card & book"; held now: "Hold {amount} & book" | no |
| Ready, consent ticked | same label | yes |
| Submitting (server call, Stripe confirm, 3DS challenge open) | "Confirming..." | no, `aria-busy="true"`, Escape does not interrupt |
| After success | button stays disabled until the browser navigates | no |

On success the page navigates to the existing `/book/confirmation?ref=…` through `getPathname({ locale, href })` and `writePurchaseSnapshot` is called first, exactly as `Step6Payment` does. The confirmation page is **not changed** in Phase 88: its heading is "BOOKING CONFIRMED" (not "paid"), and the localized card-saved email carries the payment explanation.

### Errors and notices (copy in the Copywriting Contract)

One slot, one message at a time, `role="alert"` for errors and `role="status"` for notices, text 14px/300 line-height 1.5.

| Trigger | Kind | Behaviour |
|---------|------|-----------|
| 409 `PRICE_CHANGED` | notice | Shows the server's new amount, updates the displayed amount and the Elements `amount`, **clears the consent tick**. Submit stays available after re-ticking. |
| 409 `MODE_CHANGED` (pickup crossed the 72 h boundary between render and submit) | notice | Remounts Elements in the new mode (card entry is lost), updates the explainer panel, hold date and consent text, **clears the consent tick**, moves focus to the notice. |
| Stripe card error from `confirmSetup` / `confirmPayment` (decline, insufficient funds, expired card) | error | Existing `paymentUnsuccessful` pattern using Stripe's own localized `error.message`; falls back to `paymentGenericError`. Entered card stays; client may edit and retry. |
| 3DS failed or abandoned (Stripe codes `setup_intent_authentication_failure`, `payment_intent_authentication_failure`) | error | Dedicated "bank could not verify" message. Card stays editable. |
| 422 `CONSENT_REQUIRED` | error | See consent checkbox. |
| 429 / `RATE_LIMITED`, `INVALID_REQUEST`, `LEAD_TIME`, `INVALID_PHONE`, `PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`, `INTERNAL` | error | Reuse the existing `Booking.step6.errors.*` messages and the existing `INVALID_PHONE` behaviour (message from `Booking.validation.invalidPhone`, `goToStep(5)`). |
| Network failure | error | Existing `networkError`. |
| Elements failed to load | error | Existing `paymentFormLoadError`. |

Error text colour is `#f87171` (the existing promo-error colour and Elements `colorDanger`; 6.2:1 on `#0F1D2C`). The legacy `#C0392B` is not reused for new text because it measures about 3.1:1 on the navy surface. Notice text is `--offwhite` on `--anthracite-mid` with a 3px `--copper` leading rule (same recipe as the explainer panel).

## Surface 2 — Admin booking payment panel

Admin theme tokens only. Works in light (12/14/20/28, weights 400/500) and dark (11/13/20/28, weights 300/400) exactly as Phase 86 defines them; 44px minimum targets.

### Placement and visibility

- `PaymentPanel` mounts in the expanded booking detail directly **above** `PaymentLinkSection` in both the desktop row (`BookingsTable.tsx` ~2169) and the mobile card (~2623). It fetches its own data (`GET /api/admin/bookings/[id]/payment`) when the row is expanded, so Phase 90's `BookingDetailPanel` extraction only moves it.
- It renders **only** for `payment_mode === 'card_hold'`. Exception: the "Refund failed" notice (below) renders for **any** booking whose payment endpoint reports `refund_failed: true`, including legacy pay-now bookings (D-20).
- The panel does not poll. It refetches after any action, after a trip edit (`TripEditPanel.onUpdated` bumps a `refreshKey` prop), and when the row is re-expanded.

### Panel structure

Section label above the card, same style as the neighbouring "Payment Link" label: Label token, muted. Card: `--admin-surface`, 1px `--admin-border`, radius `--admin-radius-md`, padding 16px, margin-top 16px, `data-testid="payment-panel"`.

| # | Block | Contract |

| 2 | **Alert banner** (0 or 1) | Full width, padding 16px, radius `--admin-radius-md`, leading icon 20px (`TriangleAlert` for danger/warn/amber, `Info` for info), title Body token weight strong, body Body token. `role="alert"` for danger, `role="status"` otherwise. ### Actions (HOLD-07)

Rendered **from the server's `allowed_actions` array** in the GET response; the UI never re-implements the state matrix. Expected outcome per state, for the planner's tests:

| Action (button label) | Server allows when |
|-----------------------|--------------------|
| Capture | trip `completed` and state in `authorized`, `expired`, `failed`, `card_saved` |
| Place hold now (action `retry`, before completion) / Retry charge (action `retry`, after completion) | state `card_saved`, `expired`, `failed` |
| Send payment link | state `failed`, `balance_due`, or trip `completed` with state `expired` / `card_saved` / `authorized` |
| Release hold (action `release`) | trip not completed and state in `card_saved`, `authorized`, `failed`, `expired`, `checkout_pending` |
| Waive balance (action `release` on `balance_due`) | state `balance_due` |

- Buttons: 44px high, padding 0 24px, Body token weight strong, radius `--admin-radius-md`, **neutral outline** (1px `--admin-border-strong`, transparent, `--admin-text`), gap 8px, wrap; stacked full-width below 640px. No row button is accent-filled or red; colour appears only in the confirm modal.
- No allowed action: a muted line "No actions available in this state." (no empty row).
- While any action is in flight all buttons are disabled.

**Confirm modal** (existing `Modal`, one per action; all money actions confirm, none run on the first click). Title Heading token; body Body token; amounts and the booking reference are shown so the operator confirms the exact figure. Buttons stacked on phones, side by side at >= 640px, 44px: primary then secondary.

Initial focus on the secondary (safe default for money actions). While submitting: both disabled, primary shows `LoaderCircle` + "Working…", the modal cannot be dismissed. Result handling:

| Response | Behaviour |
|----------|-----------|
| 200 `outcome: ok` | Close modal, refetch, result line "Done. The payment history is updated." |
| 200 `outcome: declined` (Stripe declined during capture / retry) | Close modal, refetch (state now `failed` / `balance_due`), result line in danger: "The card was declined. The client was emailed a payment link." |

## Claude's Choices (not decided by the owner; recorded for the planner)

1. Init failure falls open to the legacy pay-now step.
2. Hold date uses the long date with year from `formatLocaleDate`; copy says "On {holdDate} we hold…" (exact date, D-12/D-21), with no "shortly after" hedge. Research assumption A3 is resolved in favour of the exact date.
3. The consent label is plain text with the terms and privacy links in a separate line beneath, so the hashed string equals the rendered one.
4. Consent tick is cleared on any change that alters the consent text.
5. `payment_failed` is the only email with a filled button; no red in client emails; "Route" is two rows (From / To) in all emails.
6. Admin actions are rendered from a server `allowed_actions` array; every money action confirms in a modal; Release and Waive are the only red buttons.
7. The list badge appears for every `card_hold` booking (not only failures).
8. The "Owner test only" tile is the user-facing name of the `canary` mode, and no "On" tile exists until Phase 89.
9. Allowlist capped at 20 addresses, one per line.
10. Trip-edit feedback uses a `payment_effect` field on the PATCH response.

## API/Schema dependencies the UI relies on (for the planner)

- `/api/card-checkout/init` returns `{ eligible, mode, holdAtIso (Prague date source), amountMinor, currency }` and never reveals why a client is ineligible.
