# Phase 89 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Cancellation Policy, Cash Guarantee & No-Show. Eight surfaces: (1) the public localized manage-booking page, (2) the checkout additions (payment choice, cash variant, cancellation policy note, consent text), (3) the admin cancel dialog ("cancelled by"), (4) the admin fee decision (charge or waive, required note, red badge), (5) admin cash actions (charge the saved card during a trip, mark paid in cash, reverse a cash confirmation), (6) the driver cash step, (7) admin settings (policy numbers, "all clients" go-live tile), (8) client emails and operator alerts. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker. Locked inputs: `89-CONTEXT.md` D-01..D-24 (cited as D-nn), `89-RESEARCH.md` (Patterns 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), the Phase 88 contract (`88-UI-SPEC.md`: tokens, panel, badge, modal and email shell, reused not reinvented), the Phase 86/87 admin and driver tokens.
>
> **Owner decision D-16.** There is no counsel or ČOI review. The wording below is the production wording, not a draft. Every authorising string is hashed and stored per booking (`CARD_CONSENT_VERSION` and the policy version), so any later change needs a new version constant. The old "DRAFT LEGAL COPY" banner of Phase 88 is removed from the checkout, the planner replaces the `-draft-` version string with a non-draft one in the same change.
>
> **Prices and numbers.** No amount, hour count, km count or percent is typed anywhere in this contract or in code. Money is rendered from server integer minor units through `formatMinor` / `formatEUR` / `formatCZK` and ICU `{amount}`. Policy numbers are ICU placeholders (`{hours}`, `{km}`, `{percent}`, `{waitMin}`) filled from the booking's stored policy snapshot (D-24, D-18). Dates are ICU `{freeUntil}`, `{pickupDate}`.

## Surface 1 — Public manage-booking page (`/{locale}/manage/[token]`, 7 locales)

D-01..D-04. Server-rendered per request (`force-dynamic`, `robots: noindex, nofollow`), localized route (not under `(internal)`) so Arabic, Hindi and Chinese keep their fonts and `dir`. Phone first: one column, `max-w-[560px] mx-auto`, side padding 24px, bottom padding 64px. Everything reads at 320px width and at 200% text zoom with no horizontal scroll.

**Chrome.** A slim header only: the wordmark (link to home), 64px high, no site navigation, no footer links beyond the contact block below. The Nav, cookie banner, analytics and chat launcher are not mounted on this path (`isPrivateLinkPath`, see Guard Tests). `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. The page is read-only on GET; every change is an explicit POST from a button (mail scanners prefetch links).

### Cancellation panel — free variant

Surface `--anthracite-mid`, 1px `--anthracite-light` border, radius 4px, padding 24px, margin-top 32px, 3px `--copper` leading rule. Heading 20px Fraunces 400: "Free cancellation until {freeUntil}". Body 14px/300: "You can cancel this booking for free until then. {Your card hold is released. | We refund {amount} to your card. | Nothing has been charged.}" (the sentence depends on payment; for pay-now add "Your bank usually shows a refund within 5 to 10 business days.").

Below the body, `btn-secondary` (outline) "Cancel booking", full width, 44px, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` the confirm region.

### Cancellation panel — late variant

Same surface, heading "Free cancellation has ended". `TriangleAlert` 20px `--copper-light` before the heading (copper, not red: this is information, not an error). Body 14px/300 in three short paragraphs, 16px apart:

1. "Free cancellation ended on {freeUntil}."
2. "If you cancel now, {percent}% of the fare ({amount}) applies. Your booking is cancelled right away and your driver is told."
3. "A person at Prestigo decides the final charge, and may waive it, for example if your flight was cancelled. Nothing is charged until we email you."

`{amount}` is the proposed fee from the server (`feePercent` of the fare, capped at the fare).

### Cancel confirm region (inline, replaces the button; no modal, so it works on a phone and with the keyboard open)

Opens in place, focus moves to its heading (`tabIndex={-1}`), `role="group"` `aria-labelledby` the heading. Contents:

- Heading 14px/400: "Cancel this booking?"
- Summary line (outcome repeated, same sentence as the panel): free = "Nothing will be charged."; late = "{percent}% of the fare ({amount}) applies."
- **Late only:** one checkbox row exactly like the Phase 88 consent row (native checkbox, 20x20, `accent-color: var(--copper)`, whole row one `<label>`, min-height 44px, unticked on every mount): "I understand that {percent}% of the fare ({amount}) applies because I am cancelling after {freeUntil}." The confirm button stays disabled until it is ticked. This tick is sent as `acknowledge: 'late'`; free sends `acknowledge: 'free'`.
- Buttons, stacked on phones and side by side from 480px: **"Keep my booking"** (`btn-primary`, gold, receives initial focus, the safe default) and **"Yes, cancel booking"** (outline: 1px `#f87171` border, `#f87171` text on `--anthracite-mid`, 5.5:1, transparent fill, 44px; busy state shows `LoaderCircle` and "Cancelling…", `aria-busy`, both buttons disabled, Escape does not close it while busy).

### After the cancel (result region, `role="status"`, replaces the confirm region; page H1 updates to "Booking cancelled")

| Outcome | Copy (EN source) |
|---------|------------------|
| Free, hold released | "Your booking is cancelled. Nothing was charged. The hold on your card is released; your bank may take a few days to show it." |
| Free, refunded | "Your booking is cancelled. We refunded {amount}. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days." |
| Free, nothing collected | "Your booking is cancelled. Nothing was charged." |
| Late | "Your booking is cancelled and your driver is told. Nothing has been charged yet. Because you cancelled after {freeUntil}, up to {amount} may be charged. We will email you our decision." |
| Money step failed after the cancel (refund could not be made) | "Your booking is cancelled. We need to finish your refund by hand and will email you when it is done." (the client never sees an error word like "failed") |

Each result ends with: "We sent you an email." and the block-7 button turns primary.

**Cancelled summary (state `cancelled`, also on a later visit):** same surface as the panel, no confirm control. Heading 20px "Cancelled on {cancelledDate}". One status sentence from the fee case: none or free = "Nothing was charged." (or "We refunded {amount}." when a refund was made); `pending` = "A cancellation fee of up to {amount} may apply. We will email you our decision."; `charged` = "We charged {amount} for cancelling after {freeUntil}."; `waived` = "We decided not to charge a cancellation fee." Operator reasons and notes are never shown.

### Errors and notices on the page (one slot, directly above the buttons of the open region, `role="alert"` for errors, `role="status"` for notices, 14px/300, error colour `#f87171`)

| Trigger | Kind | Behaviour and copy |
|---------|------|--------------------|
| 409 `POLICY_CHANGED` (the free window ended while the page was open, or the booking changed) | notice | Region re-renders in the new variant, **clears the late tick**, focus moves to the notice: "The cancellation terms changed while you were reading. Please review and confirm again." |
| 409 `payment_busy` | error | "We are processing a payment for this booking. Please try again in a minute." |
| 409 already cancelled / status changed | notice | Page re-renders as `cancelled` or the new status, no error shown (a repeat cancel is a success to the client). |
| 429 | error | "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again." |
| Network failure | error | "We could not reach the server. Nothing was changed. Please try again." |
| Any other failure | error | "We could not cancel your booking. Nothing was changed. Please try again or call us: {phone}." (phone as a `tel:` link, `<bdi dir="ltr">`) |

### Change request (D-01)

Collapsed `<button>` "Ask for a change" (`btn-ghost`, full width, 44px, `aria-expanded`). Expanded region (inline, same surface as the panel, margin-top 16px):

- `.label` "WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO CHANGE?", then a `<textarea>` (min-height 120px, 14px/300, `--anthracite-mid` Elements-like surface with 1px `--anthracite-light` border, gold focus ring 2px, `maxLength` 1000, `rows={5}`, `autoComplete="off"`). Helper under it, 11px/300 `--warmgrey`: "Date, time, address, vehicle or passengers. We will reply by email and tell you if the price changes. Nothing changes until we confirm." - Success (`role="status"`, replaces the form): "Request sent. We will reply by email." The button is not shown again in this visit.
- Errors: empty "Please write what you would like to change." (inline, `role="alert"`); 429 "You already sent a request a moment ago. We will reply soon."; network and other "We could not send your request. Please try again or call us: {phone}."

### Invalid link view (unknown, malformed, revoked, anonymised, partner, expired)

Single view for every failure so a visitor learns nothing (pattern of `InvalidTripLinkView`). No header details, no trip data. H1 28px "This link is not valid". Body 14px/300: "Check that you copied the whole link from your booking email. If it still does not work, contact us and we will help." Buttons: email (outline), phone (outline), "Book a ride" (`btn-primary`).

### Signed-in clients: My trips (D-02)

`app/[locale]/account/trips/page.tsx` stays the same card list. For every booking whose status is not terminal (`completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`) each card gets a text-link button "Manage" (`--copper-light`, underline on hover/focus, min 44px hit area, trailing end of the card header via logical `margin-inline-start: auto`) pointing to the same manage page (token minted server-side after the ownership check). No second cancel UI exists in My trips. The four-status colour map keeps its fallback to `pending` for the Phase 87 statuses (existing behaviour, not changed here). New catalog key `Account.trips.manage` in 7 locales.

## Surface 2 — Checkout additions (public, 7 locales)

### 2.3 Cancellation policy note (new `CancellationPolicyNote`, used by card, cash and pay-now; CANC-02, D-07, D-18)

Placed directly under the explainer (card, cash) or directly under the booking summary and currency selector (pay-now), margin-bottom 32px. Surface `--anthracite-mid`, 1px `--anthracite-light` border, radius 4px, padding 24px, 3px `--copper` leading rule. `.label` "CANCELLATION". Three rows 16px apart, each a single 14px/300 sentence (the dates are the booking's own exact deadline, never "about"):

| Row | EN source | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| 1 | "Free cancellation until {freeUntil}." | Exact Prague date and time, `formatLocaleDate` plus 24h time, Latin digits in `ar`/`hi`/`zh`. The hour rule (`{hours}`) is already inside this date, so the number of hours is not repeated. |
| 2 | "After that, {percent}% of the fare ({amount}) applies. A person at Prestigo decides every charge and may waive it." | `{percent}` from the stored policy, `{amount}` = that percent of the fare via `formatMinor`. |
| 3 | "If you do not show up, the full fare applies once the driver has waited {waitMin} minutes." | `{waitMin}` is the airport threshold for airport pickups and the city threshold otherwise, from the live settings. |

A "Cancellation terms" link below the rows (`--copper-light`, new tab) points to the terms section. If `freeUntil` is already in the past at checkout (late booking), row 1 becomes "Free cancellation is not available for this trip because pickup is less than {hours} hours away." and rows 2 and 3 are unchanged.

Pay-now (`Step6Payment`): the same note is added and, directly above the pay button, one 14px/300 line: "By paying, you accept the cancellation terms above and our Terms & Conditions." with the existing terms link. There is no checkbox on pay-now (decision: acceptance by paying, recorded through the stored policy version on the booking).

### 2.4 Consent label (replaces the Phase 88 drafts; hashed and stored, three variants)

Same checkbox row, same rules (unticked on every mount, never stored client side, cleared on any change of mode, amount, currency, hold date, choice or deadline, server rejects `consent !== true`). Label 14px/300 `--offwhite`, line-height 1.5; the whole label is the stored text.

- **setup:** "I authorise Prestigo to save this card, to hold {amount} on it on {holdDate} and to charge it after my trip. If I cancel after {freeUntil}, or I do not show up, I authorise Prestigo to charge up to the full fare of {amount} to this card; a person at Prestigo decides each charge. If the price of my booking changes, I authorise Prestigo to charge or release the difference."
- **hold now:** "I authorise Prestigo to hold {amount} on this card now, to save this card and to charge it after my trip. If I cancel after {freeUntil}, or I do not show up, I authorise Prestigo to charge up to the full fare of {amount} to this card; a person at Prestigo decides each charge. If the price of my booking changes, I authorise Prestigo to charge or release the difference."
- **cash:** "I will pay {amount} in cash to the driver. I authorise Prestigo to save this card and to charge it, up to {amount}, if I cancel after {freeUntil}, if I do not show up, or if I do not pay the driver. A person at Prestigo decides each charge. No hold is placed and nothing is charged now."
- If `freeUntil` has already passed, "If I cancel after {freeUntil}, or I do not show up" becomes "If I cancel, or I do not show up" (and "if I cancel" in the cash variant).

Submit button labels: card unchanged from Phase 88; cash: "Save card & book" (ready, unticked, ticked), same busy label "Confirming…". The trust line, links line and error table of Phase 88 are unchanged; the cash choice adds no new error kinds. A `CONSENT_REQUIRED` for cash uses the same message.

### 2.5 Confirmation page

Not redesigned (Phase 88 deferred its localisation as a go-live item; the planner localises its static strings in the same change). It gains one 14px/300 sentence under the booking reference: "We sent your confirmation and a link to manage your booking to your email." Cash bookings also show "Pay {amount} in cash to your driver." ## Accessibility Contract

- **Keyboard and focus (public):** the manage page order is H1, status, details, cancellation panel, change request, new ride, contact. The inline confirm region receives focus on open (its heading, `tabIndex={-1}`); "Keep my booking" is the first control in it. Escape closes the confirm region when not busy and returns focus to "Cancel booking". Tiles use roving tabindex and arrow keys. The late tick toggles with Space.
- **Live regions:** results use `role="status"`; errors `role="alert"`; the policy-changed notice moves focus to itself. The consent invalidation announcement of Phase 88 also fires when the payment choice changes.
- **Admin:** the cancel dialog's radiogroup is `aria-required`; the outcome line is `role="status"` and updates on choice. Dialog focus returns to the invoking button; Escape closes unless busy. The red badge always carries the text "Fee decision" and the dot. Money amounts are never colour only.
- **Driver:** dialogs trap focus, initial focus on "Not yet", Escape cancels, 48px targets. The cash amount is real text, not an image.

- **Contrast:** `--offwhite` on `#17293B` about 12:1, `--warmgrey` about 6.6:1 on `#0F1D2C`, `#f87171` about 5.5:1 on `#17293B` and 6.2:1 on `#0F1D2C`. Admin and driver use contrast-tested theme tokens.
- **Motion:** only the `LoaderCircle` spinner, static under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No countdown ticks on the manage page (a ticking timer is anxiety and a screen reader hazard).
- **Zoom and reflow:** single column, wrapping buttons, 200% text zoom with no horizontal scroll at 320px.

## Claude's Choices (not decided by the owner; recorded for the planner)

1. The manage cancel confirm is an **inline region**, not a modal; "Keep my booking" is the gold, focused default and "Yes, cancel booking" the outline-red secondary.
2. A late self-cancel needs an explicit tick (D-04 "confirms"); the tick is cleared by any change of variant.
3. Pay-now has no consent checkbox: the cancellation note plus an acceptance line and the stored policy version carry the acceptance. The policy note is added to the pay-now step, which supersedes Phase 88's "unchanged" rule for that file and its test.
4. The admin cancel dialog has **no preselected "who cancelled"** (neither default can be wrong silently), and its primary stays disabled until chosen.
5. The charge dialog prefills the proposed fee and shows a live percent readout; a no-show requires a contact-attempt note, a late cancel does not.
6. The waive dialog offers three reason shortcuts ("Flight cancelled", "Regular client", "Prestigo error").
7. A declined card during a trip (cash booking, trip not completed) sends **no client email and no payment link**; the operator tells the driver to take cash (D-21). After completion the Phase 88 recovery link is used (CONTEXT Claude's discretion). The planner must make the engine suppress the link and email when the trip is not completed.
8. The driver sees the "Cash payment" chip from assignment on, the amount only at completion (D-11). The driver's "The passenger did not pay" answer notifies the operator and shows an amber banner; it creates no automatic charge, no case and no 24 h reminder (D-21).
9. "Cash unconfirmed" is an information-level state; there is no red badge for it.
10. Reversing a cash confirmation is styled accent, not red (a correction, not a destruction), and requires a note.
11. The go-live tile is a third radio tile guarded by a confirm dialog; "Owner test only" stays the name of `canary`.
12. The manage link is only in email and My trips, never on the confirmation page.
13. The change request reaches the operator as an email (`[Booking] Change request`); no admin badge in this phase.
14. Policy settings use whole numbers (hours 0 to 72, km 1 to 1000, percent 1 to 100); half-hour windows are not offered.
15. Email manage button is outlined, never filled; `payment_failed` stays the only filled button.

## API/Schema dependencies the UI relies on (for the planner)

- Manage page server data (no operator notes ever): status, `pickup_utc`, trip fields for display, policy snapshot (`version`, thresholds), the evaluated outcome (`free` | `late` | `too_late`), `freeUntil`, proposed fee minor units, payment mode and state in a client-safe form, fee case summary `{ status: 'none'|'pending'|'charged'|'waived', amountMinor }`, `holdAt`, currency.
- `POST /api/manage/cancel` `{ token, acknowledge: 'free' | 'late' }` answers `ok`, `POLICY_CHANGED` (409, with the new outcome), `payment_busy` (409), 429; `POST /api/manage/change-request` `{ token, message }`.
- `GET /api/admin/bookings/[id]/payment` additionally returns: `fee_case` (kind, status, proposed, charged, opened_at, decided_by/at, reason, contact_note, evidence), `cash` flags (`unconfirmed`, `driver_reported_unpaid`, `confirmed_by`, `confirmed_at`), `allowed_actions` including `charge_fee`, `waive_fee`, `charge_saved_card`, `mark_cash_settled`, `reopen_cash`. The admin bookings list payload carries `fee_case_status` so the red badge needs no extra request.
- `POST /api/admin/bookings/[id]/fee` is the only place an amount enters (strict schema, cap = `payment_amount_minor`, currency from the row); `outcome: 'ok' | 'declined'`; 409 `amount_locked`.
- Admin cancel route body gains `cancelled_by: 'client' | 'prestigo'` (required).
- Driver trip progress route gains `confirm_cash` and `cash_not_received` actions; the trip page selects the cash fields and builds the amount label server-side only for unsettled cash bookings.
- Settings route and `pricing_globals` carry `cancel_free_km_threshold`, `cancel_free_hours_short`, `cancel_free_hours_long`, `cancel_free_hours_hourly`, `cancel_fee_percent`; `card_flow_mode` accepts `on` (migration 087 and `lib/payments/config.ts`).
- Payment state `cash_settled` and ledger kinds `fee_opened`, `cash_settled` (and `cash_reopened` if the planner models the reversal as its own kind) are added together with the labels in Surface 5.
- Consent: new mode `cash_setup`, non-draft `CARD_CONSENT_VERSION`, the three labels of Surface 2.4 keyed by `consentSetup`, `consentHoldNow`, `consentCash`.
