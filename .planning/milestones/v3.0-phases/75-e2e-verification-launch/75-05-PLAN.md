<objective>
Thread the booking locale through the payment stack (D-07, D-11): Stripe Elements and Google Places follow the site locale, the locale is persisted on the PaymentIntent (and so on the ABND booking capture input), and the Stripe webhook forwards it into all three server-side GA4 purchase events; the Stripe return URL keeps the user in their locale.

Purpose: today every non-English booking renders Stripe's payment form and Places suggestions in English (hardcoded), and server-side purchases carry no locale, so revenue cannot be split by language.
Output: `lib/booking-locale.ts` maps, locale-aware Step6Payment/AddressInputNew, `metadata.locale`, `Ga4PurchaseParams.siteLocale` wired through the webhook, with tests.
</objective>
