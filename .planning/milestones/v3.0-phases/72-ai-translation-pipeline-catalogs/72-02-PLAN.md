## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-72-02 | Tampering | prompt injection / model deviating from DNT rules in translated output | medium | mitigate | Fail-closed post-hoc verifier (Task 2) — any unit that drops/alters an ICU var, tag, DNT term, or plural category is flagged, not shipped; independent of why the model deviated |
| T-72-06 | Tampering | translated string breaks next-intl ICU parse at render time | high | mitigate | verifyDntPreserved rejects missing/unbalanced ICU tokens before a unit is accepted, preventing a render-time throw in a translated locale |
