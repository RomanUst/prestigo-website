| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-76-38 | Spoofing | restored instance contacting customers | high | mitigate | outbound blocked at the provider firewall and DOCKER-USER before apps start (restore.sh gate); Sidekiq and EspoCRM daemon never started; in-container egress probe must fail |
